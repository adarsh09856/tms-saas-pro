A clearer way to coordinate transport.

Kodewaves TMS gives each transport company a workspace for planning, bookings, dispatch, delivery visibility, and day-to-day coordination.

Designed around real work

Transport work crosses teams. Bookings, customer contact records, vehicles, drivers, schedules, proof of delivery, and finance all affect one another. Kodewaves TMS connects those records so staff can follow each job with context.

Transport companies manage their own staff and operational records. Customer accounts are not required: company staff create bookings and can share public tracking updates.

Built for transport operations

The platform brings company setup, bookings and dispatch, fleet maintenance, driver operations, finance and reporting, and warehouse stock into one system, with role-based access for each team.
