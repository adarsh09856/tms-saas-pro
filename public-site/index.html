Who creates an order?

Authorized staff at the transport company create and manage orders in their company workspace.

Do customers need a login?

No customer portal is part of this TMS. A public tracking page can show the shipment information the transport company chooses to share.

What appears on a company domain?

A verified company domain opens that company's authenticated staff workspace and approved public and tracking pages in the same Kodewaves TMS. Platform Admin stays on the central host.

Can every company use its own look?

Company branding applies to its public company site and supported communications. Platform administration and operating controls remain centralized.
